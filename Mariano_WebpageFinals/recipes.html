Pork Sisig

Crispy sizzling pork seasoned with calamansi, onions, and chili peppers.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork belly
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 chilies, chopped
  • 3 calamansi
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Boil the pork until tender.
  2. Grill or fry until crispy, then chop.
  3. Sauté the onion and chili in a hot pan.
  4. Add the pork, season, and squeeze calamansi on top.

Lechon Cebu

Roast pig stuffed with lemongrass, garlic, and spring onions.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly
  • 5 stalks lemongrass
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 bunch spring onions
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Season the inside with salt, pepper, and garlic.
  2. Lay lemongrass and spring onions on top, roll tightly, and tie.
  3. Rub the skin with salt and dry it in the fridge overnight.
  4. Roast until the skin is golden and crackling.

Pastil

Shredded chicken over rice, wrapped in a banana leaf.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 300 g chicken breast
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • Banana leaves

Steps

  1. Boil the chicken, then shred it.
  2. Simmer the shreds with onion and soy sauce until dry.
  3. Soften a banana leaf over a flame.
  4. Add rice, top with chicken, wrap tightly, and serve.
